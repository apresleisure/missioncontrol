
2. brand-voice.md (The "LeisureVerse" Identity)
Save this at ~/Apres-Leisure-Brain/wiki/brand-voice.md.
Summary: Defines the "Apres" aesthetic and the mythic, minimal, and slightly mysterious tone of the brand.
	•	Core Tagline: "The adventure changes. The feeling never does. Every day is Apres." 
	•	The Philosophy: We focus on the "moment after"—the ritual following the climb, the run, or the long day.
	•	Keywords: Ritual, Signal, Apres, Afterglow, The Third Place, Mythic, All-Terrain, All-Access, Lore, Legend, Apres Alter Ego. Some Alpine references are ok because that's where this was born (Wasatch Mountains).
	•	Anti-Voice: Avoid "hypebeast" noise, "crypto-bro" energy, or generic "tourist shop" or "AI slop" messaging.
	•	Visual Cues: mountain flame shaped logo featuring "The First" (sasquatch) embedded in the "A" shape. Alternate logo is neon glowing bar sign spelling Apres Leisure.
